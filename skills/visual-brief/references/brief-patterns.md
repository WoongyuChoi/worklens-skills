## Reader hierarchy

1. Conclusion.
2. At most three pieces of evidence.
3. One action or unresolved question.

Unknown is a valid result. Never manufacture a progress percentage.
For a plain CLI, prefer short numbered steps or narrow tables over a diagram that cannot render.
For a visual file, keep body text at least 16px and key labels at least 18px; prioritize whitespace and meaningful hierarchy over decorative cards.
